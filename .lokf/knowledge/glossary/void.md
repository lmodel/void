# VoID

VoID, the Vocabulary of Interlinked Datasets, is an RDF vocabulary for describing a dataset of linked data as a whole: who made it, how to reach it, what it holds and which links join it to other datasets. Its terms are named under `http://rdfs.org/ns/void#`, written `void:`. This repository's schema renders it with VoID's own names and IRIs.[^readme.md]
