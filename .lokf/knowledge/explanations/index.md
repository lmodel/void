# Explanations

* [Names and IRIs](names-and-iris.md) - Why the schema keeps VoID's names and IRIs (`void:Dataset`, `void:distinctSubjects`) and the Dublin Core, FOAF and RDF Schema ones, puts only what it adds under `void_linkml:`, names the partition classes as it does, relaxes the slot naming rule, and leaves `dcterms:date` out.
* [Scope, influences and mappings](scope-and-influences.md) - What the void schema models and leaves out, the one influence beyond VoID (lmodel/slsa's release asset and governance files), and its mappings: `sd:Dataset` and `sd:Graph` as narrow and `dcat:Dataset` as broad for `Dataset`, `foaf:Document` for `DatasetDescription`, and `void:Dataset` for the partitions.
