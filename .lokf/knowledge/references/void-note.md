# Describing Linked Datasets with the VoID Vocabulary

The Note is a W3C Interest Group Note of 3 March 2011, published through the Semantic Web Interest Group. Its authors are Keith Alexander, Richard Cyganiak, Michael Hausenblas and Jun Zhao. It explains how VoID describes a dataset's general metadata, its access, its structure and its links, how to publish a description, and how to discover one.[^note]
